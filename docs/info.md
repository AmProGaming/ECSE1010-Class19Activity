## How it works

Project takes in 2 inputs (A and B), puts them into an AND gate, and puts that output into an LED.

## How to test

Press the run button in the top left, then switch switches A and B on or off to test the AND gate and LED output.

## External hardware

An LED is used to display the output of an AND gate.
